Sambar

40 min

Description

A tangy, tamarind-based lentil stew cooked with mixed vegetables and a fragrant tempering of mustard seeds and curry leaves. A staple alongside rice, dosa, and idly.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup toor dal (split pigeon peas)
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (drumstick, carrot, pumpkin)
  • 1 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 2 tbsp sambar powder
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • A few curry leaves
  • 2 dried red chilies
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt, to taste

Steps

  1. Pressure cook the toor dal until soft and mashable.
  2. Simmer the mixed vegetables in water with tamarind paste and sambar powder until tender.
  3. Add the cooked dal and combine, adjusting water for consistency. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. In a small pan, heat oil and add mustard seeds, dried chilies, and curry leaves — let them sputter.
  5. Pour the tempering over the sambar and mix well.
  6. Serve hot with rice, dosa, or idly.
