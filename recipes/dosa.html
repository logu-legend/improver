Dosa

30 min + overnight ferment

Description

A thin, crisp crepe made from a fermented rice and urad dal batter. Cooked on a hot flat pan until the edges turn golden and lacy. Served with chutney and sambar.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice
  • 1/2 cup urad dal (split black gram)
  • 1/4 tsp fenugreek seeds
  • Salt, to taste
  • Water, as needed
  • Oil or ghee, for cooking

Steps

  1. Soak rice, urad dal, and fenugreek seeds separately for 4–6 hours.
  2. Grind into a smooth batter, combine, and let ferment overnight (8–12 hours) until bubbly.
  3. Add salt and mix well. The batter should be pourable but not watery.
  4. Heat a flat pan and pour a ladle of batter, spreading it thin in a circular motion.
  5. Drizzle oil around the edges and cook until the base is golden and crisp.
  6. Fold and serve hot with chutney and sambar.
