Idly

20 min + ferment

Description

Idly (also spelled Idli) is a soft, fluffy, steamed rice cake that is one of the most beloved and traditional breakfast dishes of South India. Simple yet deeply satisfying, idly has been a staple morning meal for generations across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala, and Andhra Pradesh.

Ingredients

  • Idly rice / parboiled rice
  • Urad dal (skinned black lentils)
  • Poha (flattened rice)
  • Fenugreek seeds
  • Salt
  • Water

Steps

  1. Soak rice with fenugreek seeds for 6 hours & urad dal with poha for 4 hours.
  2. Grind urad dal into smooth fluffy batter, rice into slightly coarse batter.
  3. Mix both batters together, add salt.
  4. Ferment overnight or 8–10 hours in a warm place until batter doubles.
  5. Grease idly moulds with oil or ghee.
  6. Pour batter into moulds and steam for 10–12 minutes, rest 2 minutes, scoop out with a wet spoon.
