Briyani

1 hr 15 min

Description

Biryani is a royal, aromatic rice dish that has been cherished for centuries across South Asia and the Middle East. It is a celebration on a plate — layers of fragrant long-grain basmati rice cooked together with tender meat, vegetables, or eggs, all infused with a bold blend of whole and ground spices.

Ingredients

  • Basmati rice, water, salt, bay leaves, cloves, cardamom, cinnamon
  • Chicken, yogurt, lemon juice, garlic & ginger paste
  • Onions (sliced)
  • Tomatoes
  • Green chilies
  • Oil or ghee
  • Spices
  • Garnish

Steps

  1. Soak rice for 30 minutes, then drain.
  2. Marinate chicken with yogurt, spices, garlic & ginger — rest 1 hour.
  3. Fry onions in ghee until golden and crispy.
  4. Cook chicken in the same pot with tomatoes and chilies until 80% done.
  5. Par-boil rice with whole spices until 70% cooked, then drain.
  6. Layer rice over chicken, top with mint, coriander, saffron milk, fried onions.
  7. Dum cook — seal the pot, high heat 5 min, then lowest flame for 20 min.
  8. Rest for 10 min, gently mix, and serve hot with raita & salan.
